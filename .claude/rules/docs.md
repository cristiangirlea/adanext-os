# Plan, status and backlog

- `docs/plan.md` is the accepted starting plan. Don't rewrite its decisions. A material change goes
  in the decisions log of `docs/implementation-status.md` with the date and the reason.
- `docs/implementation-status.md` records each milestone as not started, in progress,
  implemented, or verified. `verified` needs the exact revision, the commands that ran, and their
  results; an agent's report alone is not evidence. Say plainly what did not run.
- `docs/backlog.md` lists slices with their owned files or interfaces and acceptance tests. Tick a
  slice with its pull request and date when it merges.
- Model availability and quotas change: record the date of every observation.
